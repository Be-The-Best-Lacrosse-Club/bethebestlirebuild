The Lab at Momentum Sports

Team Strength Training

Led by owner Quintin Germain. Open to BTB teams 2033 and older.

Teams train together as a full roster — functional, athletic movement built to develop power, force, and motor control, so we come out of the winter as more resilient, more explosive athletes.

WHO
BTB teams 2033 and older (5th grade and up), boys and girls.
PRICE
16 sessions — $500 per player. Need more or fewer sessions? Quintin will adjust the plan and the cost to fit the team.
MINIMUM
10 players per team. Under 10 and the price goes up, so commit early.
SCHEDULE
Slots directly before or after your team's practice hour. Practice at 9:00 AM? Lift at 8:00 or 10:00, then you're done for the day.
GYM
Private access — each team has the floor to themselves. No sharing, no distractions.
WHERE
Momentum Sports · 10 Dunton Ave, Deer Park, NY 11729
PAY & REGISTER — $500
Secure checkout through QuickBooks. Card or bank accepted.

Straight Answers

Parents Ask.
We Answer.

What ages does BTB serve?

Boys travel teams for birth years 2037–2030 and 2028, girls travel teams for birth years 2037–2031, plus BTB Futures for grades K–2. Every age group trains under the same development system.

Where do you train and play?

We're based on Long Island, NY. Training runs at Plainedge High School and BTB East at St. Joseph's University in Patchogue, with tournament travel across the Northeast.

Are there current roster opportunities?

Yes. A small number of openings are available for committed players. Visit our Players Wanted page to see current teams and contact the appropriate coach.

What makes BTB different from other Long Island clubs?

Weekly film study for every player, small-group skill training, a written coaching standard, and character development built into every practice. Development is the product — wins are the evidence.

01 — The Foundation

One Program. One Language.

A single curriculum, top to bottom, boys and girls.

The biggest change this year is one I have wanted to make for a long time. I have built a full curriculum where the same terminology is used across the entire program — every team, every age group, offense and defense.

That means a 2033 hearing a concept in practice is hearing the exact same word our oldest teams are hearing. When a player moves up, nothing is foreign. When a coach subs in, nothing is lost in translation. And when we get to a tournament, every team on the sideline is speaking the same language.

This curriculum flows down to every coach and every staff member, and it ties directly into our film sessions so players hear it, then see it, then talk about it.

  • Offense — shared concept names, shared spacing language
  • Defense — shared calls, shared slide language
  • Rides & Clears — one system across the club
  • Subbing — one standard, so it looks clean at every level

The result you will see on the field: our kids are going to look more prepared than they ever have.

02 — Film Study

Film Sessions on Zoom

Short, focused, and recorded so nobody falls behind.

I will personally be running grouped film sessions for both the girls and boys programs. Each session walks through the terminology, shows clips of the concept, and then we open it up for discussion with the players.

I know everybody's schedule is packed. That is exactly why these are intentionally brief:

Younger Age Groups

15–20 Minutes

Terminology, clips, and a short discussion. Tight and age-appropriate.

Older Age Groups

20–25 Minutes

Deeper concept work with more player-led discussion of what they are seeing.

EVERY SESSION IS RECORDED

If your player misses one, you will not be behind. All sessions will be recorded and posted where you can click and watch on your own time. I will let you know exactly where they live once they are up.

It is important that we get these in. They are the connective tissue between what we say at practice and what happens on Saturday.

Some teams are on a path that will require travel. Other teams we are still navigating toward tournaments where they will be successful, enjoy playing with their teammates, and get the right kind of adversity to grow through. Both paths are intentional.

10 — Gear & Resources

Equipment, Stores & Logos

Get this squared away before the first practice back.

BEFORE PRACTICE RESUMES

We pick things up with practices right after Labor Day. Please go through your player's bag this week. The goal is simple: they get out of the car, get their gear on, and are ready to practice. No equipment problems eating into field time.

  • Stick is ready — pocket is legal and it is throwing well
  • Helmet fits — properly, not from two seasons ago
  • No equipment issues — anything broken, handle it now

If your player needs a new stick or you want advice on what to buy, message me directly. I am happy to help you out. Equipment is extremely important — to play well, a kid has to feel comfortable.

COLD WEATHER

It gets cold, and it gets cold fast. Please make sure everyone has proper clothing. It is always easier to take warm clothes off than to stand out there freezing. Keep a hoodie and sweatpants in the bag at all times.

GIRLS — GLOVES

If your daughter wears gloves, they must be lacrosse gloves. Not winter gloves. And hands do not go inside sleeves. If you need help finding the right pair, let me know.

TEAM STORES

Team stores are mostly optional. I know the boys' and girls' stores should be separate and I am going to work toward that going forward.

If there is a specific item you want in the store, send a message to me, Sean, or Taylor. We will pass it to Lacrosse Unlimited, they will source that article of clothing, and let us know whether we can use it.

LOGOS & CALENDAR

  • Vector logo files — I am putting a link on the website. If you are running a fundraiser and need logos, pull them from there. Please only use the logos in that file.
  • Master calendar — I am providing a calendar you can view. The coaching staff uses it as the source of truth, and then we update TeamSnap from it.
11 — Standards

Communication Is the Driving Force

How we handle problems at BTB.

If you are unhappy with something, please bring it to the coaches. They bring it to me or to the director, and we fix it. Too often things fester quietly until they are past the point where we can do anything about them.

Let us know when there is an issue and we will help find a solution. That is the whole standard here: we find solutions, we do not complain, and we do what is best for the kids.

THE 24-HOUR RULE — NO WARNINGS THIS YEAR

Last year we had issues with parents approaching coaches at tournaments and ignoring the 24-hour rule. There will be no warning this year.

If you are unhappy at a tournament, you wait 24 hours. You do not approach a coach. If you do, I have to remove you from the program.

This is one of the standards we are holding ourselves to. It is not fair to the coaches, and we are all adults. BTB has always tried to be the club that keeps that standard, and we all want to function together so the kids are successful on and off the field.

A full set of policies will go out next week, once all the team Zooms have concluded.

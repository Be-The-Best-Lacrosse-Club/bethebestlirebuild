Good To Know

When does it launch?+

We're building now and onboarding founding members first. Join the waitlist to lock in early pricing and get access as soon as content goes live.

Is this for boys, girls, or both?+

Both. The platform has separate, full curriculums for the boys and girls games — different rules, different concepts, taught properly for each.

Do I need to be in the BTB club to use it?+

No. It's open to any player, coach, or club who wants to develop lacrosse IQ — though BTB families get special access.

What makes this different from free YouTube?+

Organization and teaching. Instead of hunting through random clips, you get original breakdowns mapped to every concept, tiered by age and position, with tools to study and assign — built by coaches who win.

Can my whole team or club join?+

Yes — Coach and Club plans cover teams and full programs with shared curriculum and coach onboarding. Reach out for program pricing.

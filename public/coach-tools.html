Drill Library.

Use drills that match the phase, the team need, and the next game problem. Avoid filler reps.

Stickwork

Wall Ball Ladder

Right, left, alternating, quick-stick, cross-hand, and on-the-move throws. Finish with pressure catches.

Defense

Slide + Recover

4v3 or 5v4. Force direction, communicate hot, slide on time, fill inside, recover on pass.

Transition

4v3 Break

Push pace, read the first slide, move the ball twice, finish before the defense gets matched.

Boys Specific

Clamp + Exit

FOGO technical reps with wing reads. Clamp, rake, exit, protect, and start the break.

Girls Specific

Draw + Wing Read

Draw specialist placement with two wing reads. Win possession, secure, and attack space.

The BTB Standard.

Every coach should sound aligned. These are the non-negotiables that shape every practice and every sideline.

01

Effort

No walking between reps. Sprint to huddles. Finish ground balls. Compete through the whistle.

02

Accountability

Correct the behavior, then teach the fix. Standards are only real when they are enforced calmly and consistently.

03

Pace

Short lines, fast transitions, clear segment ownership, and water breaks that teach rather than drift.

04

Communication

Players must call ball, help, hot, two, release, and reset. Coaches should demand volume and clarity.

05

Film Loop

Game issues become practice priorities. Practice corrections become film checkpoints the next week.

06

Safety

Equipment checked, contact controlled, hydration managed, and age-appropriate contact at all times.
